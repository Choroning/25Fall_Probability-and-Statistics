| [HW01_Probability-and-Random-Variables](HW01_Probability-and-Random-Variables/) | Handwritten solution and explanation of assignment 1, given in week 7. It covers unknown constants in a probability table, conditional probability, independent events, Bayes' theorem, the Poisson, hypergeometric, and binomial distributions with the normal approximation, the distribution of a maximum and linear transformations, the relation between $E[X^2]$ and the variance, the joint cumulative distribution function and the distinction between uncorrelatedness and independence, and a concept map of Lectures 2-5. | [HW02_One-Sample-Z-Test](HW02_One-Sample-Z-Test/) | R solution and report of assignment 2. On clinical data of 8,921 subjects, it tests the systolic blood pressure of the whole sample with a two-sided one-sample Z-test (Q1) and that of COVID19 patients with a right-tailed test (Q2), compares tests of male heart rate in three directions at the 5% and 10% significance levels (Q3, Q4), and compares resting oxygen saturation with Korean health checkup statistics (Q5). It connects to Lecture 6 (Hypothesis Testing) | [Assignment explanation](HW02_One-Sample-Z-Test/Assignment-Explanation.md), [Code walkthrough](HW02_One-Sample-Z-Test/Code-Walkthrough.md), [R code](HW02_One-Sample-Z-Test/Solution/) |
| [HW03_Nonparametric-Tests-and-Multiple-Testing](HW03_Nonparametric-Tests-and-Multiple-Testing/) | R solution and report of assignment 3, given in week 14. It compares the COVID-19 case counts of two occupation groups with a pooled two-sample t-test and a Wilcoxon rank-sum test and chooses the appropriate test (Q1, Q2), and performs an exact permutation test with the Seo statistic, the absolute difference of medians, and discusses its limitations (Q3, Q4). For 1,274 genes it repeats Welch t-tests between the cancer and healthy groups (Q5) and Pearson correlation tests with height (Q6), corrects for multiple testing with the Bonferroni and Benjamini-Hochberg methods (Q7), and describes the meaning and use of the results (Q8). It connects to Lecture 6 (Hypothesis Testing), Lecture 7 (Statistical Analysis), and Lecture 8 (Nonparametric Approaches) | [Assignment explanation](HW03_Nonparametric-Tests-and-Multiple-Testing/Assignment-Explanation.md), [Code walkthrough](HW03_Nonparametric-Tests-and-Multiple-Testing/Code-Walkthrough.md), [R code](HW03_Nonparametric-Tests-and-Multiple-Testing/Solution/) |
| [Lab01_Introduction-to-R-Programming](Lab01_Introduction-to-R-Programming/) | Code from the R practice class of week 2 (9/8). It covers creating vectors and `class()`, the structure and summary of the iris data, `rep()` and `seq()`, plotting normal random numbers made with `rnorm()`, data frames and factors, and a ggplot2 box plot, and connects to Lecture 1 (Introduction to R Programming) | [Lab_R-Basics.R](Lab01_Introduction-to-R-Programming/Lab_R-Basics.R) |
